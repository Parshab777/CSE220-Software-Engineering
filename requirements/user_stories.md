## Sprint 1 Plan

- **Duration:** 2 weeks (Aug 17 – Aug 30, 2026)
- **Sprint Goal:** Deliver the core borrowing workflow — search, borrow, return, and catalog management.
- **Committed stories:** #1, #2, #3, #4, #5 (19 points total)
- **Deferred to Sprint 2:** #6–#10 (reservation, member management, reporting, fines, admin dashboard)

## Trello Card Fields

Each story above should be entered as a Trello card with:
- **Description:** the user story text
- **Label:** priority (Red = High, Yellow = Medium, Green = Low)
- **Custom field / checklist item:** story points
- **Due date:** matching the story's sprint
- **Assignee:** responsible team member
